This spell calls hidden things into the light. It is worked to reveal truth, expose deception, uncover secret motives, and bring clarity where shadows and silence have clouded the path. May what is concealed be seen, and may justice rise beneath the watchful flame.

## You'll Need

- One large pillar candle
- 4 smaller candles
- A fire safe dish for burning a slip of paper
- A mirror
- Paper and pen
- Mugwort
- Rosemary
- Thorny branches or leaves of any kind
- Florida water or Justice Oil (any peppermint or cinnamon oil will also work)
- A bell or set of keys

Place the candle in the center of a dark room. Black, white, or blue work beautifully. Set the mirror underneath the candle. In folk magic and witchcraft, mirrors have long served as tools of revelation, reflection, and truth telling. In this spell it also represents you as the one whose light will shine through the darkness

Surround the main candle and mirror with the thorny branches. These represent the lies or injustices that are attempting to dim your light. In the dark they srround the mirror that represents the portal to you

Hold the pillar candle and think about the strengthening sun. the warm days. Think about the tumultuous spring, cold and painful, then warm and balmy. Begin to relate that metaphorically to the situation you want to bring justice or truth to.

Imagine the candle is a representation of the power of the sun itself

It has the power to bring light to darkness, illuminate that which is obscure, burn away deceit, and nourish the blooms that rise from the dirt and the dark.

Hold those thoughts as you light the pillar candle and say something like:

> “By the growing strength of summer light,  
> I call illumination into this working.  
> What is concealed shall be seen.  
> What is hidden shall be known.”

Then light the center candle

Sit quietly for several moments, breathe and allow yourself to settle.

Gaze at the reflected light and thorns in the mirror and focus on the situation that feels clouded, thorny, or concealed. Think about it calmly and clearly. Do not spiral into anger or extreme emotion. This spell is not about revenge. It is about revelation and justice.

Take the rosemary in your hands and crumble it slightly between your fingers as you sprinkle it in a small dish and say:

“By rosemary, let my mind be clear.”

Take the mugwort and sprinkle it in the dish and say:

“By mugwort, let my sight go deeper than appearances.”

## Add a thorn and say

“By thorn and bramble, may truth defend itself.”

## Add 9 drops of Justice oil and say

> “With this oil, infused with the Illuminating powers of the sun,  
> I call the Fires of Justice, Truth, and transparency  
> To bring clarity and restore balance”

With the herbs and oil, anoint each candle, place them in the four directions (or corners), and light them one at a time, beginning in the East and moving clockwise around the central flame in the shape of a square. If it helps to visualize the square, you can draw a square in chalk, herbs, or salt with the candles in each corner.

![Light in Dark Corners Spell](/assets/img/uploads/wix-light-in-dark-corners-spell-2.webp)

The image of the squared circle strengthens this spell because the entire working is built around bringing hidden imbalance back into alignment. The circle and the square are two of the oldest sacred symbols in mystical traditions, alchemy, ceremonial magic, and folk spirituality alike. Together, they represent the meeting point between spiritual truth and material reality.

When combined, the squared circle becomes a symbol of balance between the unseen and the seen. Between intuition and proof. Between spiritual knowing and earthly justice.

You are asking for hidden truths to move from the invisible realm into the visible one. From whispers into words. From shadow into sunlight.

As each candle is lit, envision the growing square of light pushing back both physical darkness and the hidden shadows surrounding the situation.

- East / Air
> “By breath and whisper, by wind and sacred thought,  
> let truth illuminate lies and scatter deception like smoke.”

- South / Fire
> “By my will and by sacred flame,  
> I bring light to the unknown and burn through all illusion.”

- West / Water
> “By tide and tear, by river and rain,  
> may justice flow where silence once gathered.”

- North / Earth
> “By stone and root, by bone and buried things,  
> let what was hidden rise into the light and stand revealed.”

Write on the paper what you believe is being hidden or kept from justice. You do not have to know the exact truth. You can write things like:

> “What is concealed from me?”  
> “Who is acting dishonestly?”
> “What truth needs to come forward?”  
> “May justice be seen.”

Fold the paper once toward yourself if you seek clarity for your own understanding. Fold it away from yourself if you seek exposure of someone else’s deception.

- Hold the paper near the flame of the central candle and say:
> “Fire that sees through shadow,  
> Flame that burns away illusion,  
> Illuminate what festers unseen.”

Light the paper from the pillar candle and let it burn in the burn dish and say:

> “Let truth rise like smoke.  
> Let justice move like thunder.  
> What has been concealed may now be revealed.”

Now gaze softly into the candle flames. Let your thoughts drift naturally. Watch for feelings, flashes of memory, symbols, names, or sudden realizations. You may receive insight immediately, but often this spell works through unfolding signs over the next days or weeks. Pay attention to dreams, overheard conversations, slips of the tongue, strange timing, sudden confessions, or information surfacing unexpectedly.

When you feel finished, say:

> “What is hidden cannot remain buried forever.  
> Truth has a voice.  
> Justice has a path.  
> The dark corners are no longer empty.”

Ring the bell or shake the keys to dispel any lingering negativity

Thank the energies you’ve raised, then extinguish the candles.

Have something to eat or drink, take a few deep breaths of fresh air, walk barefoot in the grass and feel the sun on your face, or some other grounding and centering exercise to come back to equilibrium, once the working is complete

Bury the thorns or keep them on your altar to continue to guard you. Burn the spell remains to continue the theme of illumination, or throw them away to set the intention of ridding yourself of any negative energy (for example if you did the spell to expose someone who is slandering you or trying to unjustly accuse you of something)
