On our Fashionably late Ostara episode we talked about this spring-time tarot spread that we both fell in love with! Try it and let us know what you think!

1 The Soil

Where do you grow from, what lessons can be learned from the past? What are your roots?

2 The Weed

What are your obstacles, and what is holding you back? What is stunting your progress?

3 The Seed

Your immediate future, in what area will you first see signs of growth? What seeds have taken hold and will begin to grow?

4 The Sun

This is partly the love and support you get from other people; but remember that the will to grow comes from within. What nourishes you? What do you need to keep in mind in order to help yourself grow? Energy and lessons from other people that you internalize and use to help you move forward.

5 The Rain

A blessing in disguise. Things are not always what they seem. If the skies are grey and rainy you may miss the sun but rain is just as necessary as the sun to grow. Through struggle, you gain strength. What blessing in disguise is on your horizon?
