The wheel turns towards summer, witches! And with the warming of the weather here in the USA comes the start of festival season 2025! Ah what could be better than the sound of drums in the distance, the scent of incense and fire smoke in the air, and the gathering of magickal souls under open skies and cozy tents!?!?! Spend the summer with other witchy souls setting up your altar in a forest clearing, dancing barefoot at sunset, and hopefully hugging those online witch friends in real life! Mark your calendars and pack your cloaks, because the witches are wandering, and the magick of festival season is calling!

## Paganicon 2025

**Dates:** March 21–23, 2025

**Location:** Plymouth, Minnesota

**Description:** An annual conference that brings together individuals interested in Paganism and earth-based spirituality. The event features workshops, rituals, and musical performances, offering a space for community building and spiritual exploration.

[tcpaganpride.org/register](https://tcpaganpride.org/register/)

## Sacred Space Conference 2025

**Dates:** March 27–30, 2025

**Location:** Towson, Maryland

**Description:** An annual esoteric conference on the East Coast for intermediate to advanced practitioners. The event offers a variety of workshops and rituals from qualified teachers, fostering continuous development among attendees from diverse magical traditions. [sacredspacefoundation.org](https://sacredspacefoundation.org)

## Austin Witchfest 2025

**Date:** March 29, 2025

**Location:** Austin, Texas

**Description:** A celebration dedicated to the education and practice of witchcraft. The festival includes workshops, vendors, and rituals, providing attendees with opportunities to learn and connect with the witchcraft community.

[austinwitchfest.org](https://austinwitchfest.org/)

## Rites of Spring 2025

**Dates:** May 20–25, 2025

**Location:** Western Massachusetts

**Description:** An annual gathering celebrating the sacred Earth. The event includes rituals, workshops, music, and community-building activities, providing a space for individuals to connect with nature and each other in a spiritually enriching environment

[earthspirit.com/events/rites-of-spring-47](https://www.earthspirit.com/events/rites-of-spring-47)

## The New York Faerie Festival

**Date:** June 27, 28 & 29, 2025

**Location:** Sherman, NY

[nyfaeriefestival.com](https://www.nyfaeriefestival.com/)

## Mystic South 2025

**Dates:** July 11–13, 2025

**Location:** Atlanta, Georgia

**Description:** A conference that explores various aspects of mysticism, paganism, and alternative spirituality. The event includes workshops, presentations, and rituals, fostering a space for learning and community building.

[mystic-south.com/mystic-south-2025](https://mystic-south.com/mystic-south-2025/)

## WitchsFest USA 2025

**Date:** July 12, 2025

**Location:** New York City, New York

**Description:** An annual street fair that celebrates witchcraft and paganism. The event features workshops, vendors, and rituals, offering a platform for education and community engagement.

[witchsfestusa.org](https://witchsfestusa.org/)

## Starwood 2025

**Date:** July 15-21

**Location:**

Reawaken, Renew, and Expand at The Starwood Festival

America's Longest Running Transformational Festival

[starwoodfestival.com](https://www.starwoodfestival.com/)

## Sankofa Festival 2025

**Dates:** July 18–20, 2025

**Location:** Sherman, New York

**Description:** Held at the Brushwood Folklore Center, this festival celebrates the creative spirit with workshops, ceremonies, community fires, and performances. It offers participants opportunities to explore various pathways, engage in creative activities, and connect with the community.

[brushwood.com/sankofa](https://brushwood.com/sankofa/)

## Sirius Rising Festival 2025

**Dates:** July 21–27, 2025

**Location:** Sherman, New York

**Description:** Also at the Brushwood Folklore Center, this week-long festival features workshops, rituals, musical performances, drumming, and dancing. It fosters creative and spiritual growth, bringing together a diverse community to celebrate various traditions and practices.

[brushwood.com/siriusrising](https://brushwood.com/siriusrising/)

## Dragonfest 2025

**Dates:** July 30th – Aug 3rd

**Location:** the mountains of Colorado

**Description:** Dragonfest is a large, regional, neopagan spiritual retreat held in the mountains of Colorado each August. People of all religions and spiritual traditions are welcome at Dragonfest, but workshops and rituals focus on various Neopagan, Wiccan, and other Earth-based spiritual practices.

[dragonfest.org](https://dragonfest.org/)

## TempleFest 2025

**Dates:** August 29 – September 1, 2025

**Location:** Charlton, Massachusetts

**Description:** Hosted by the Temple of Witchcraft, TempleFest is an annual summer festival celebrating Pagan and Witchcraft traditions. The four-day event includes workshops, rituals, and community-building activities, providing a space for spiritual exploration and connection.

[templefest.templeofwitchcraft.org/presenters](https://templefest.templeofwitchcraft.org/presenters)

## Hexenfest 2025

**Dates:** October 9–12, 2025

**Location:** Upper Lake, California

**Description:** A music and arts festival for witchy creatives and visionaries. The festival explores themes of the otherworldly, animistic, mythic, pagan, and fairytale, providing a space for inspiration and community.

[hexenfest.net](https://www.hexenfest.net/)

## Twilight Covening

**2025** **Dates:** October 10–13, 2025

**Location:** Western Massachusetts

**Description:** Organized by the EarthSpirit community, this three-day intensive focuses on deepening earth-centered spiritual practices. Participants join affinity groups led by experienced mentors, engaging in rituals, workshops, and activities that foster personal and spiritual growth.

[earthspirit.com/events/twilight-covening](https://www.earthspirit.com/events/twilight-covening)
