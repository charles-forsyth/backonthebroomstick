While chatting with the brilliant Lalania Simone on a recent episode of Back on the Broomstick, Laylla and Chelle discussed her beautiful Spiritual Growth Spread that she created for Tarot of the Cosmic Seed. Lalania revealed that she adds more cards than the book shows and gave us permission to let you all in on her secret! Here is the modified "Spiritual Growth Spread" by Lalania Simone !

1 The questioner

2 & 3 The Root (added by Lalania during episode) - Why are we here in the present? What events have impacted or are impacting the current question?

4 The spiritwork at hand. What is blooming or unfolding for your spiritual growth?

5 & 6 These cards indicate the environment and the support/challenges that may arise to the work.

7 & 8 Indicates short term progress as you do the work

9 The outcome or what needs to be worked on further
