Our story

Once upon a chilly fall of '93, Laylla and Chelle crossed paths on an upstate New York college campus, bonding over Spades and Magic: The Gathering. Their long discussions and mystic adventures grew into a magical sisterhood. They studied the craft together, trained with a diverse group of practitioners, and their monthly rituals, spellcraft and meditation sessions soon drew a core group of witches.

A couple of years later they founded a full coven and tradition, Circle Amaurot. They taught witchcraft classes locally through the coven and CUUPS, took workshops and seminars on tour from Salem to Savannah, and led large rituals and events in the wider pagan community. Three decades later, they are still stirring the cauldron together.

The podcast

Like a lot of podcasts, this one was born in the pandemic, when even witches swapped broomsticks for Zoom links. Separated by 2,500 miles and a few life detours, they had both drifted from their practice. With Laylla's techno-wizardry and Chelle's gift of gab they conjured The Stoned Witches Hour in 2022: paranormal stories meet cannabis 101 meet witchy tips. Like any good spell it evolved, and in February 2023 it became Back on the Broomstick, a weekly ride for anyone yearning to reconnect with their inner witch.
